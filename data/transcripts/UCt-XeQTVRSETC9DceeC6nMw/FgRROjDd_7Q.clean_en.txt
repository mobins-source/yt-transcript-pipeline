Descend into prostration, placing his hands on the ground before his knees." Some scholars say the opposite, that he places his knees before his hands. And this is the most correct view. And this is what the majority holds. The majority of scholars Hold that he places his knees before his hands. But there is no harm in this or that. If he places his hands before his knees , or his knees before his hands, there is no harm. But what is forbidden is to descend into prostration a camel. The Prophet, peace and blessings be upon him, says: "None of you should kneel a camel kneels." Meaning, to drop down forcefully, descending from standing to prostration without composure. So if he can do it on his knees, it is more correct, and it is what the majority holds. If he can do it on his hands before his knees, there is no harm. But he should not descend a camel, descending without composure. This hadith, the saying of the Prophet ( PBUH) "None of you should kneel a camel kneels," does not mean descending on the knees or the hands, but rather the manner of descending. One should not descend, whether on his knees or his hands, unless he is composed. He says: "Then he would say takbir and descend into prostration, placing his hands on the ground before his knees, and he would rely on his palms, spreading them out, keeping his fingers together, and directing them towards the Qibla. He would place them level with his shoulders, and sometimes level with his ears, and fix his nose and forehead on the ground."
